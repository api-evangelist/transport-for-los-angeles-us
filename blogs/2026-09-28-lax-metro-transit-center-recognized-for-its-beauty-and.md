Metro’s LAX/Metro Transit Center has been named one of the World’s Most Beautiful Stations for 2026 by Prix Versailles, an international architecture and design awards program. Click here to see all the winners. The station is one of seven worldwide — and the only one in the United States — selected for this year’s list.
