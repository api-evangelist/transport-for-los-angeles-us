Metro’s values are centered on the people who rely on the system every day. Hence, Metro has officially released its FY27 Adopted Budget with a focus on delivering a transit experience that is safer, cleaner, more reliable, and easier to use. The newly published budget puts riders first through targeted investments across the entire network.
