The long-awaited, eye-catching Lucas Museum of Narrative Art opens Sept. 22 in Exposition Park, bringing together paintings, illustrations, comics, photography, movie costumes, and Star Wars artifacts—all focused on the art of visual storytelling. As with many other destinations in Exposition Park, the Museum is easy to reach by Metro.
