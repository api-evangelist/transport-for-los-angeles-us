The Metro Board of Directors approved funding for a variety of transportation projects and programs across Los Angeles County at its September meeting. These include street and school-zone safety improvements, better walking and biking connections and other multimodal projects. Here’s a rundown: More than $5 million for walking and biking improvements in northern L.A.
